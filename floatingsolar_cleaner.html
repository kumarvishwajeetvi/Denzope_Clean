Core Mechanical & Embedded Architecture

01 / MECHANICAL LOCK

Dual C-Clamp & Nested Wheels

Wraps around both edges of the solar panel aluminum frame with nested rollers underneath—guaranteeing zero slip or drop-off during reservoir wave tilt.

02 / SCRUBBING MATRIX

5-Brush Staggered Underside

Five rotary soft microfiber pads arranged in an overlapping matrix deliver 100% glass coverage to clear baked guano without scratching anti-reflective coatings.

03 / FLUID SYSTEM

Filtered Micro-Mist Pump

Draws and filters reservoir water for targeted misting prior to brushing—preventing mineral scaling while conserving water and energy.

04 / EDGE INTELLIGENCE

ESP32 + IMU Wave Guard

Triggers dawn cleaning cycles when soiling ratio drops below 0.90 (avoiding midday thermal shock) and automatically halts runs during high-wave events.

05 / TELEMETRY

LoRaWAN Long-Range Alerts

Transmits cleaning logs, battery health, and reservoir telemetry across multi-megawatt floating solar installations with ultra-low power draw.

06 / AUTONOMOUS DOCK

Zero-Touch 24V LiFePO4 Dock

Self-aligns across full array rows and returns automatically to its solar trickle-charging dock upon cycle completion.
